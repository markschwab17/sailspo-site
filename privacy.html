SailSpo

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to run your trip, and nothing for advertising.
  • No tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of your data with data brokers.
  • Your trip is visible only to the crew aboard it, unless you choose to share a route publicly.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

SailSpo is made by Mark Schwab. Contact: markschwab17@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, or your Apple ID sign-in (Apple may give us a private relay email), and an account ID.
  • Profile: the name, photo and optional phone number you choose to show your crew.
  • Trip content you add: stops, itinerary, notes, comments, votes, lists, documents, photos and videos, expenses and receipts. Receipts you scan are read on your phone to fill in the total.
  • Home city and home airport (optional): used only to estimate your flight cost. Only the city and airport code are stored, never a street address. Your crewmates can't see them; captains see only a price range across the crew.
  • Places and routes you share (optional): community places you add, and routes you choose to publish.
  • Notification token, if you allow push notifications, so we can send trip updates.

SailSpo does not read your device's location, contacts or photo library. Photos are chosen with Apple's picker, and the app only writes to your library when you tap Save.

Who can see it

  • Your crew: everything on a trip is visible to the people aboard that trip. Captains can manage the trip and its crew.
  • Everyone, only if you choose: places you add can become public after we review them. Routes you share show the route, its stops, times and notes, the boat type and length, the month, and the credit name you type. They never include crew names, photos, money, dates or the trip's name.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI trip planning

"Plan with AI" uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. What you type and the plan it drafts stay on your phone. Only the stops you choose to add are saved to your trip.

Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage. On the developer's own private trip, full-size photo originals are also archived to his personal storage; no other trip's photos go there.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, Maps search and Apple Intelligence.
  • Cloudflare: stores trip photos and videos, and runs the live room for the in-trip game on some trips.
  • Ignav: looks up flight prices. It receives only airport codes and dates, never who you are.
  • Garmin ActiveCaptain: shows community notes about anchorages and marinas. If you sign in to ActiveCaptain to write a review, that happens with Garmin under Garmin's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account), we delete your sign-in, profile and profile photo, and any trip where you're the only one aboard. Things you added to a trip other people are still on, like photos, comments and expenses, stay with that trip for the crew but are no longer linked to you. Routes you shared publicly stay public, credited to "A sailor". You can unshare a route before deleting your account.

Children

SailSpo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, remove anything you've added, and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your data or anything else about your privacy, email markschwab17@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
