SailSpo

A trip planner for sailing crews: the route, the crew, the photos and the money, in one place.

Support

Questions, problems, or something in the app that shouldn't be there? Email markschwab17@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within two days.

Report content

Every shared place and community route in the app has a Report button. You can also email markschwab17@gmail.com with the name of the place or route. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours and removed if it breaks the Terms.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. It deletes your sign-in, your profile and photo, and any trip where you're the only one aboard. If you captain a trip with other crew, hand the captaincy to someone first. You can also email us to ask.
